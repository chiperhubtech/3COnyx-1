Maintain and enhance the current 3C / CUXTOMCANDYCLUB platform exactly as it is, without removing, rebuilding, or breaking any existing features, workflows, monetization systems, payout systems, referral systems, or user experience.

Primary objective:
Strengthen the platform by making it AI-native, data-driven, and future-ready for advanced technologies (including NVIDIA-level capabilities such as AR/VR visualization, predictive intelligence, and digital simulation), while preserving speed, simplicity, and founder control.

---

CORE RULES:

1. DO NOT BREAK OR REBUILD
- Preserve all current logic, flows, UI, and systems
- Only enhance by adding layers, not replacing existing structure

2. PRESERVE SPEED + EXECUTION
- Maintain fast deployment and iteration

- Keep Replit-first workflow fully functional

  - Vehicle details

- Design system so it can later plug into predictive AI models

5. VISUALIZATION PREPARATION (AR/VR READY)

  - “Preview upgrades”
  - “Visualize parts/customizations”

Create a platform that:
