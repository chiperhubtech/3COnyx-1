The "Top Tech" Perspective: Why this works
 * Event-Driven vs. Linear: Standard apps wait for you to click. Event-driven apps (like yours) watch the data. The moment a bank approves a loan in your system, the Logistics Agent already knows to start looking for a transport driver. That’s how you get to "2 minutes to resolution."
 * Stateless & Scalable: By telling Replit to build "Stateless Services," you ensure that whether you have 10 users or 10 million, the app doesn't crash. Each request carries its own context.
 * Confidence Scoring: This is the "Secret Sauce." By forcing the AI to give a confidence score, you protect yourself legally. If the AI isn't 90% sure about a finance contract, it must ask a human. That's how you win the trust of the "Startup Warriors" and the Banks.
